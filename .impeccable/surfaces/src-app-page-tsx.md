# Surface: whole storefront (home, shop, product, checkout, nav, footer)

Mode: Persuade (home) + Operate (shop, product, checkout). Audience: Karachi homeowners redoing one wall; action is add to cart or book a free measure. Proof: stock room photos and the hero film only; no invented projects, years, stats or reviews.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The storefront is a gallery of walls, not a grid of boxes. Full-bleed rooms, giant thin uppercase type and hairline rules carry the structure; it refuses the rounded-card grid and the boxed stat/feature tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Night chapters on #13100e charcoal and day chapters on a cool stone #e7e4df, ink #1b1612, one clay accent #b95e37 for the primary action only. Geist 200–400, display uppercase tight, labels uppercase 11–12px tracked 0.16em. Square-cornered photos, 1px hairlines, pill primary buttons, underline secondary links.
STORY: See the finished room (hero film), pick a material from the collection index, see it in room ideas, buy it from a hairline product gallery, book the free measure.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed film; LATH & LINE across the top-left at 13vw; tagline under it; clay pill + underline link; category index top-right; bottom hairline bar with delivery, scroll, pause. Premium three-zone nav floats transparent above.
FORM: Code-led; world pinned by the user's two reference sites and the approved hero (no concept roll: brief-pinned). Signature interaction: the collection index, huge type rows with a cursor-following room photo (GSAP quickTo).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
